AGENTIC STUDIO - WINDOWS QUICK START

WHAT YOU RECEIVE
AgenticStudio-1.0.0-win-x64.zip is a portable app, not a Setup.exe installer.
Python, the backend and Chromium are included. No Docker or commands are needed.
Keep AgenticStudio.exe, _internal and BrowserCompanion together.

OPEN THE APP
1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All. Choose a permanent folder.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click AgenticStudio.exe.
3. Keep the small status window open. Your dashboard opens in your browser.
4. Create a local account. Open API connection and add your own OpenAI API key.
5. Choose Open a new browser, describe your task and start. No extension is needed
   for this option. Review the results, then Save and download Excel, CSV or JSON.
Complete website sign-in, CAPTCHA or missing details when prompted.
If your development dashboard is already running, close it first.

USE YOUR EXISTING CHROME OR EDGE TAB - OPTIONAL
1. In the app's status window, click Extension setup to open the included folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions in your browser.
3. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select BrowserCompanion.
   Disable any older developer copy first. You do not need the separate ZIP.
4. Keep the dashboard signed in in that browser. Open your website tab, open
   Browser Companion, describe your task and click Start.
The extension is included in the download but is added to the browser separately.

WHERE IT RUNS
The backend starts automatically on your own computer, not the developer's PC.
Your prompt and captured page content go to OpenAI over the internet.
API usage is billed to your configured account.
Accounts/results: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AgenticStudio\data.
Use Open saved-data folder in the status window to find them.

Keep the app and dashboard tab open during tasks. Stop the task, then choose
Stop and close in the status window when finished. Closing a browser tab alone
will not stop the app. Back up your saved-data folder separately.

This is an unsigned pilot package awaiting manual verification.
